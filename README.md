# 📊 Data Portfolio

Welcome to my data portfolio! Here I document a summary of my projects and case studies in the data field.

## Data Analytics projects


| Project Link | Completion Date | Tools | Project Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| [🎫 IT Service Desk Automation](https://github.com/VishwajaMane04/IT-Service-Desk-Automation-Analysis) | Jun 2026 | Python, PostgreSQL, Power BI | Analysed 41,927 service desk tickets to identify automation opportunities and quantify avoidable workload. Found 65.8% of tickets (~27,600) were avoidable, consuming 4,759 analyst hours (~1.4 FTE, ~£38.7K/year). Recommended self-service password reset, a KB deflection chatbot, and automating top call drivers to cut ticket demand ~15%. |
| [🛒 E-commerce Sales Funnel](https://github.com/VishwajaMane04/ecommerce-sales-funnel-analysis) | Jun 2026 | BigQuery (SQL), Power BI | Analysed 4,268 visitors across a 5-stage purchase funnel to locate revenue leakage and channel inefficiency. Identified View→Cart as the main bottleneck (only 31% progress) while the lower funnel converts above 70%, and found email converts at 34% vs social at 7%. Recommended shifting spend upstream and away from low-converting social traffic. |
